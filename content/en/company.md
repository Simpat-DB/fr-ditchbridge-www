Ditchbridge was born from a field observation: after fifteen years spent organising major sporting events in complex, fragmented environments, its founder saw how much operational teams lacked a tool truly designed for their work — **a tool able to support them in the moment**, not simply to document what has already happened.

## A company built by and for operations

Ditchbridge was founded by Simon Patard, who spent ten years in the event operations of Paris Saint-Germain and then three years at the organising committee of the Paris 2024 Olympic and Paralympic Games. He was later joined by Charles Sabourdin, a senior software architect specialising in security and the production deployment of critical systems. This dual culture — event operations in the field and demanding software engineering — underpins Ditchbridge's positioning: **building a tool from the real needs of operational teams, rather than adapting a generic tool to the world of events.**

## Strong academic roots

Ditchbridge was built in close collaboration with the research community, in particular with the Industrial Engineering Centre of IMT Mines Albi and the work of Frédérick BENABEN, Sébastien REBIERE and Nicolas SALATGE, which gave rise to the technology that inspired the platform. The company is supported by the IMT Mines Albi incubator and the Lanceur d'Étoiles programme, and maintains exchanges with several support organisations in Occitanie. **These roots give Ditchbridge continuous access to the latest advances in artificial intelligence applied to decision-making and the modelling of complex systems.**

## A clear position in a fast-growing market

The market for operational command platforms is growing steadily, driven by the digitalisation of sites, the rise of artificial intelligence applied to operations and stricter safety and security requirements. **This market is still fragmented today: existing players each focus on a particular segment — incident management, access control, mapping, timing — without any of them covering the whole operational lifecycle, from preparation to lessons learned.** This is precisely the space Ditchbridge intends to occupy, as a unified command-support platform tailored to complex organisations and fragmented environments.

## An ambition beyond events

While events are Ditchbridge's original field of deployment, the real-time management of complex, multi-stakeholder systems that the platform enables is intended, in time, to extend to other sectors facing the same critical coordination challenges — energy, water or transport. **Ditchbridge is developing along a progressive path, firmly focused on operational excellence and European technological sovereignty.**
